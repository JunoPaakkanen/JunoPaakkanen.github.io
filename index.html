The most objective ranking of characters from the anime Mato Seihei No Slave / Chained Soldier based on my personal opinions.

Brief introduction to Mato Seihei No Slave

The series is set in a near-future Japan where portals to a dangerous dimension, called Mato, have appeared across the country. In response, the government establishes the Mato Defense Force (MDF), a special military unit composed of superpowered women, to protect humankind and fight the dimension's inhabitants. The story follows Yuuki Wakura, a young man who accidentally wanders into Mato and is rescued by Kyouka Uzen, a top-ranking MDF officer.

The manga officially began serialization on January 5, 2019 and is still ongoing, written by Takahiro and illustrated by Yohei Takemura. An anime television series adaptation, animated by Seven Arcs, aired from January to March 2024. A second season, animated by Passione and Hayabusa Film, aired from January to March 2026.
